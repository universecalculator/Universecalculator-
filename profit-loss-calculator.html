Understanding Profit and Loss

Profit: The financial gain when selling price exceeds the cost price.

Loss: The financial loss when cost price exceeds the selling price.

Profit/Loss Percentage: The ratio of profit/loss to the cost price, expressed as a percentage.

Markup: The amount by which the cost of a product is increased to determine the selling price, expressed as a percentage.

Margin: The ratio of profit to revenue (selling price), expressed as a percentage.
